INCIDENT DOCUMENTS

CTR Duplicate Checker

Check your filed reports across all batches. Select the folder containing all formatted CTR PDFs, including newly received reports, to find matching employee or crew names and work dates.

Analyze formatted PDFs

Choose one copy to keep in each matching group. Open the reports and confirm which files are duplicates before removing them. Reports with different contents are flagged for review.

Checks PDFs directly in the selected folder. Subfolders are not scanned. Removal is permanent and requires your confirmation.

Formatted filenames

Employee_or_Crew_CTR_YYYY-MM-DD_to_YYYY-MM-DD.pdf

Single-date reports are supported. Capitalization, spaces versus underscores, and numbered copy suffixes are ignored when matching. Both work dates must match. Unrecognized filenames and unreadable groups are skipped.

Use the separate CTR File Renamer to format each incoming batch, then run this checker on your full filed folder.

Offline utility · No accounts · No network requests · Everything stays on this computer
